FileTwin — duplicate & document checker

Find your files' twins.

FileTwin scans any folder or drive for byte-for-byte duplicate files, plus something most tools miss: files with the same name but content that's drifted apart — like a letter saved twice with a different closing line. It tells you which copy to keep. You decide the rest.

What it finds

01

Exact duplicates

Every file is hashed and compared — two files only count as duplicates if their content matches exactly, byte for byte. Works across any number of folders or drives at once, so you can compare a backup drive against your main one directly.

02

Same name, different day

Files that share a filename but aren't identical get compared on content similarity — the classic case is a document re-saved in a different folder with small edits. FileTwin flags how similar they are and recommends keeping the most recently modified copy.

03

You decide

Nothing is deleted automatically. Every group shows a recommended copy to keep, with checkboxes for the rest. Removal goes to the Recycle Bin by default — permanent deletion requires a deliberate second confirmation.
